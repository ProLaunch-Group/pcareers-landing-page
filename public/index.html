Who We Are

Launching Careers. Building Confidence.

At ProLaunch Careers, we believe that talent is equally distributed, but opportunity is not. We are a career grooming organization dedicated to equipping early-career African professionals with the skills, tools, and strategic positioning required to land competitive roles in the global remote workforce.

We operate at the intersection of professional coaching and digital strategy. Whether you are a skilled professional struggling to get past automated screening systems, or an aspirant ready to pivot into a remote career, we provide the roadmap to get you there. From our 14-day Career Grooming Camp to our proprietary AI CV optimization tool, LaunchIQ, we don't just offer advice—we deliver measurable career acceleration.

Our community is powered by a belief in African excellence and a commitment to real-world results. Having coached over 2,000 alumni to date, we are building a generation of professionals who are ready to compete and win on the world stage.
