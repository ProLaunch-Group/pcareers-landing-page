🎙️ Meet Your Masterclass Speakers

Joyce Ubani

Founder, ApplyPadi
Speaker 1

Joyce is a Career Strategist and the founder of ApplyPadi, a career platform helping African talent access job opportunities and support to advance their careers. Through her work, she supports job seekers in navigating the job market, presenting themselves effectively to employers, and positioning themselves for career opportunities. She is dedicated to employability and workforce development, with a focus on preparing African talent for the evolving world of work.

Cornelius Abeken Christopher

Business Development Strategist
Speaker 2

Cornelius Abeken Christopher is a Business Development and Strategic Partnerships professional with over five years of experience driving business growth, technology adoption, and strategic collaborations across Africa. He has collaborated with tech companies, businesses, and Pan-African organizations—including the African Union and UNECA—to advance digital transformation, workforce development, and economic opportunity. Cornelius is dedicated to leveraging innovation and strategic partnerships to unlock business opportunities and accelerate sustainable growth throughout the continent.

Coach MQ (Mary-Queen)

Founder, ProLaunch Careers
Host

Leading Career Strategist, Remote Work Consultant, and Founder of ProLaunch Careers. Coach MQ has equipped over 2,000 African professionals as a career coach with the positioning, clarity, and confidence they need to break barriers and secure high-impact roles in the global remote workforce.
