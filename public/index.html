Who We Are

Empowering Remote Professionals

ProLaunch Careers is a career grooming organization focused on helping early remote professionals secure high-paying jobs through structured coaching and strategic branding.

We offer personalized coaching, 14-day career bootcamps, and digital products designed to help skilled, stuck professionals gain clarity and land opportunities.

Through our structured curriculum, we provide a holistic career acceleration experience:

Our Services

Career Grooming Bootcamp: A 14-day intensive program for skilled professionals who lack recognition.
1-on-1 Coaching Sessions: 15-minute consultations to fix career bottlenecks and create roadmaps.
Professional Branding: CV rebuilding, LinkedIn optimization, and online authority.
Remote Job Focus: Targeted at professionals looking to earn in foreign currency.

Meet the Visionary Behind Your Career Launch

Mary-Queen Uchechukwu (Coach MQ) is a Career Development Coach, Cloud & DevOps Engineer, and Senior Business Operations Professional with over 8 years of cross-sector experience spanning technology, operations, and human capital development.

She is the Founder of ProLaunch Careers, a platform built to bridge the gap between talent and opportunity for early-career and remote professionals across Africa and beyond. Through Coach MQ, she delivers structured, high-impact career programmes designed to equip professionals with the clarity, confidence, and competitive edge they need to launch and grow in today's fast-evolving job market.

Coach MQ brings a rare combination of technical depth and people-first coaching to everything she does. As a practising Cloud & DevOps Engineer, she understands the real demands of the modern workplace, and as a certified trainer and facilitator who has trained over 2,000 professionals, she knows exactly how to close the gap between where you are and where you want to be.

Her career grooming approach is rooted in lived experience: Coach MQ has navigated multiple industries, built businesses, and mentored professionals navigating career pivots, remote work transitions, and first-time entry into competitive fields. She has led training programmes, designed bootcamps, and worked with organisations at the strategic level, bringing a boardroom-to-bootcamp perspective that few coaches can offer.

Coach MQ doesn't just teach careers, she builds them. If you're ready to stop waiting and start launching, you're in the right place.
