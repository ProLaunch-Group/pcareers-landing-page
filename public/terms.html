2. Description of Services

ProLaunch Careers provides educational programs, professional mentorship, career consulting, and resume optimization tools designed to equip professionals with skills for remote and global employment. Our core offerings include:

  • Career Grooming Camp (CGC): A 14-day intensive cohort-based training program strictly capped at 50 participants per cohort.
  • ProLaunch Mentorship Initiative (PMI): A volunteer-based, virtual mentorship program matching early and transitioning professionals with experienced mentors.
  • Career Optimization Services: Human-led CV rewrites, LinkedIn branding, portfolio guidance, and AI-powered evaluation tools.
